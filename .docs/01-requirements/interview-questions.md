# EventMFU — User Interview Questions (Google Form)

**Purpose:** Validate pain points P1–P9 before building. Each question maps to at least one pain code so findings can be traced directly to backlog items.
**Target respondents:** MFU students (any school, any year) — both those who attend events and those who have organized or helped run one.
**Estimated time:** 5–8 minutes.

## Google Form setup notes

1. **Make Q11 a branching question** — respondents who answer "No, and I'm not interested" skip to Section 5.
2. **Make Q8 a branching question** — respondents who answer "No" skip Q9.
3. **Q18** — use the Grid / Matrix question type (rows = features, columns = 1–5 scale).
4. **Required fields:** Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4, Q7, Q11, Q12, Q16, Q17, Q22. All others optional so the form doesn't feel burdensome.
5. **Form title suggestion:** *"MFU Student Events — Quick Survey (5 min)"* — avoid naming EventMFU to keep responses unbiased.
6. **Add a short intro at the top:** *"We're researching how students at MFU discover and join university events. Your answers help us understand what's working and what isn't. No personal data is collected beyond your school and year."*
